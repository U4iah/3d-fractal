Pareidolia lab

A blind self-report experiment on this fractal. Each trial shows one configuration (mirror type, view angle, roll) for a few seconds in random order, without telling you which. Tell it what you see; it logs your answer next to measured image statistics. Small samples only describe you; findings about 2D noise do not automatically hold for 3D fractals.

3D mesh export · experimental

Experimental. Samples this fractal's distance field on a grid and extracts a surface you can save as OBJ or STL. It is an approximation: detail finer than one voxel is lost and thin features can vanish. The result is a closed offset shell of the fractal and is not checked for printing (no wall thickness or overhang analysis). A ray-marched picture is not a mesh.
